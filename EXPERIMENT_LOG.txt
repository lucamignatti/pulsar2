How to compare a new job
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Need [DIST][COLLECT] lines with nreal, target, ticks, infer_s, env_s, prep_s, record_s,
collect_s, pool=. Rank-0 means of Overall / Collection / Consumption SPS after skipping
iter 1. Note numGames, pool size, whether old/nexto chances are zero, pipeline on/off.
Do not compare Length()-fill collect SPS to fixed-step (overshoot vs exact 200k).
Do not treat Dist Sync as "NCCL is slow" until per-rank collect times are in the log.
Do not compare scale jobs that still used tsPerSave as a global-timestep
interval (4630607-0613): auto-save joined the collect worker every 1-2 iters.
iterPerSave=40 is the save cadence from 4630618 on.
Checkpoints belong on scratch-shared, never barn-shared (fileset fills, link fails).
